Frequently asked questions
What's the difference between a Discipline Toolbox and a Premium Tool?＋
Discipline Toolboxes ($99/mo) cover the everyday tracking, calculation, and reporting work for one discipline. Premium Tools ($500/mo each) generate the actual drawing or GIS/CAD deliverable — plan sheets, permit packages, plats — and are priced separately because of what they replace.
Can I combine tools across disciplines without buying All-Access?＋
Yes — subscribe to as many Discipline Toolboxes and individual Premium Tools as you need. All-Access is just the flat-rate option once you're using most of them.
Is there a seat limit, or can my team share a login?＋
Discipline Toolbox and All-Access plans support team accounts — no per-seat penalty for adding your crew.
Do you offer a free trial?＋
Yes — a 3-day free trial on any plan, from the button at the top of this page.
What export formats do the tools support?＋
Depends on the tool — CAD tools export DXF, GIS tools export Shapefile/KMZ/GeoPackage, and tracking tools export CSV/XLSX/PDF. Every tool exports in the format your downstream workflow already expects.
Do I need to install anything or involve IT?＋
Not for most tools — they run in the browser, no VPN or IT ticket required. A handful of Premium Tools install as a lightweight AutoCAD or ArcGIS add-in, since that's where the deliverable actually lives — those are called out on each tool's card.
